Kameri BG now works with CarPlay – plus lots of new features

Published October 25, 2026

The biggest Kameri BG update yet is here. The app now runs on your car's display with CarPlay, tells you what speed to hold for the rest of a zone, warns you with a voice in Bulgarian, and answers Siri.

At a glance

  • CarPlay support
  • A safe speed for the rest of the zone
  • Alerts about 1 km before every zone
  • Spoken alerts in Bulgarian
  • Siri and Shortcuts
  • A new map, more accurate zone detection and saved history

CarPlay

Kameri BG now works on your car's display, with three tabs:

  • Drive – live speed, average speed, the limit, distance to the end of the zone, time, and a reset button.
  • Zones – the zones nearest to you first, then every zone by road. Each shows its distance, limit and length.
  • History – your recently completed zones.

CarPlay is a one-time purchase of €4.99, unlocked from the side menu in the iPhone app. Restore Purchase is available too.

A safe speed for the rest of the zone

Inside a zone, the app tells you whether you're on track. If you're over the limit, it shows the exact speed to hold for the rest of the zone so you finish within it.

If that's no longer possible, it tells you that your average will stay above the limit. It never suggests driving faster than the limit.

The safe speed appears on the main screen, in CarPlay and on the Lock Screen (Live Activity).

Alerts before every zone

About 1 km before a zone starts, you get a notification (also in CarPlay) and a spoken alert with the limit. It only triggers when you're heading into the zone in its direction.

Spoken alerts in Bulgarian

A voice announces:

  • when a zone is coming up;
  • when it starts;
  • when it ends, with your average speed;
  • when your average goes over the limit, with the speed to slow down to.

Music is lowered while it speaks, and it works with the screen locked. You can turn it on or off and hear a sample in the settings.

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask hands-free, including in CarPlay:

  • What's my average speed in Kameri BG
  • How far to the end of the zone in Kameri BG
  • Reset average speed in Kameri BG

Siri answers out loud without opening the app. The spoken commands are in English, since Siri doesn't support Bulgarian.

A new map and more accurate zones

  • A new full-screen map with floating glass panels for the title, your stats and the map controls.
  • A better "follow me" camera: it tilts, looks ahead of the car and turns with your direction. If you move the map, it pauses, and it resumes after 10 seconds or with the recenter button.
  • More accurate zone detection, based on the actual road geometry and your direction of travel.
  • Zones awaiting certification are now shown too, as a purple dashed line, so you can tell them apart from certified zones (solid blue). The zone you're driving is highlighted orange.

Corrected zone data

  • The Trakia Vakarel ↔ Ihtiman zone now follows the motorway and ends at the right camera.
  • The I-8 Ihtiman ↔ Mirovo limit is corrected to 90 km/h.

Trips and history

  • A reset button on the trip card, with confirmation. Resetting the trip doesn't interrupt a zone you're currently driving through.
  • The trip resets automatically after 30 minutes stopped, so every drive starts fresh.
  • History is now saved and survives restarts:
    • Recent groups your drives by day;
    • By zone shows how often you've driven each zone, how many times you were over the limit, and your overall average there.
  • You can share or clear your history.

And more

  • Welcome screens on first launch explain why location access is needed before asking for it, then offer notifications.
  • Better battery life: the screen stays on only while driving, charging or connected to CarPlay, instead of always.
  • New Alerts settings for the voice, zone-ahead alerts, zone start and end, and an average-speed threshold.
  • A cleaner launch screen that matches the new design.
  • Fully translated into Bulgarian, including CarPlay and the purchase screen.
