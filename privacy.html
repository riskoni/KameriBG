Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the Kameri BG mobile app handles your information. In short, it doesn't collect any.

1. Information we collect

Kameri BG does not collect or transmit any personal data. Neither the app nor the developer tracks you in any way.

Your trip history is stored only on your device. It is never sent to the developer, and you can clear it at any time in the app. If you choose to share your history, it goes only to the people or apps you pick.

2. Location access

The app uses your device's GPS to determine which speed zone you are in. Your location is processed only on your device and is never transmitted, stored or shared.

3. Internet connection

Kameri BG works entirely offline, so your data stays on your device.

The only exception is in-app purchases, such as CarPlay support, which are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your personal or payment information. If you use Siri, your requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

4. Data sharing

Because Kameri BG does not collect any data, there is nothing to share with third parties.

5. Children's privacy

Kameri BG does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Since the app collects no data, major changes are unlikely. Any updates will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at prostyle.app.feed@gmail.com.
