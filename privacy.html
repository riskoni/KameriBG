Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2025

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the Kameri BG mobile app handles your information. In short, it doesn't collect any.

1. Information we collect

Kameri BG does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Neither the app nor the developer tracks you in any way.

2. Location access

The app uses your device's GPS to determine which speed zone you are in. Your location is processed only on your device and is never transmitted, stored or shared.

3. No internet connection

Kameri BG works entirely offline and never connects to the internet, so your data always stays on your device.

4. Data sharing

Because Kameri BG does not collect or store any data, there is nothing to share with third parties.

5. Children's privacy

Kameri BG does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Since the app collects no data, major changes are unlikely. Any updates will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at prostyle.app.feed@gmail.com.
