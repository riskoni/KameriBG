Kameri BG / Камери БГ

Know the speed limit on every stretch of Bulgaria's motorways, and drive past average speed cameras without worrying about a fine.

Simple, private, reliable

Average speed cameras measure your speed over a whole section of road, not at a single point. Kameri BG keeps you informed the entire way.

📍

Your zone and a safe speed

See the limit for the section you're on, and the exact speed to hold to finish the zone within it.

🚗

CarPlay

Live speed, average speed, nearby zones and history, right on your car's display.

🔊

Spoken alerts in Bulgarian

A voice warns you before each zone, when it starts and ends, and if your average goes over the limit.

🎙️

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask for your average speed or the distance to the end of the zone, hands-free.

🔒

Offline and private

No internet needed. Nothing is collected or tracked, and your trip history stays on your phone.

🆓

Free to download

Get it free on the App Store. CarPlay support is an optional one-time purchase.

See it in action

A clear, distraction-free view of the limit that applies to you right now.

New in version 2.0

Live updates on your lock screen

Follow your average speed and the safe speed for the rest of the zone from the Lock Screen or the Dynamic Island, without opening the app.
