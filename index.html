Kameri BG / Камери БГ

Know the speed limit on every stretch of Bulgaria's motorways, and drive past average speed cameras without worrying about a fine.

Simple, private, reliable

Average speed cameras measure your speed over a whole section of road, not at a single point. Kameri BG keeps you informed the entire way.

📍

Your current speed zone

Uses your phone's GPS to show the speed limit for the section you're driving on.

📴

Works fully offline

No internet or mobile data needed, so it keeps working in areas with poor coverage.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Nothing is collected, tracked or sent anywhere. Everything stays on your phone.

🆓

Free to use

Download it from the App Store at no cost.

See it in action

A clear, distraction-free view of the limit that applies to you right now.

Coming in version 1.2

Live updates on your lock screen

Follow your average speed from the lock screen or the Dynamic Island, and get notified right when it matters, without opening the app.
